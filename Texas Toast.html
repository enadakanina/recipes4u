Texas Toast

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 10 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients:


Bread(4 slices)

Seasoning(garlic, salt, pepper, parsley)

Softened Butter

Cheese (Motzarella and Parmegan)

Instructions:

Toast 4 slices of bread. Than mix the butter with garlic powder, salt, pepper and parsley. After you mixed them spread the butter on the pieces of toast. Shred the motz and parm chesses and put that on the bread too. Now put them in the oven and bake it at 180 degrees celcius until the cheese is melted. Enjoy!
