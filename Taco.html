Chicken Taco

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 30 minutes

Servings: 5 servings

Ingredients:


5 tortillas
Chicken breasts
Garlic
Chicken taco seasoning
2 ripe avocadoes
Tomato
Onion
Parsley
Salt& pepper
11/3 cup water
Lemon Juice
11/3 cup water

Instructions:

To make these delicious tocos start by cutting the chicken breasts into strips and adding them to a pan along with a clove of garlic and a pack of chicken taco seasong. Mix this well and then add about 1/3 of a cup of water. Let this cook on low heat while you make the guacamole. Mash 2 ripe avocadoes with 1 finely cut red onion, tomato, parsley, S&P and lemon juice. Now you can put this in a blender or leave it chunky, depends on the preference. After the chicken is cooked you're going to dip one tortilla in the water and put it on a pan. After a few seconds, flip it and on only one side add cheese, the chicken and guacamole on top. Close the torilla and cook on both sides until the crust is crispy. Enjoy!
