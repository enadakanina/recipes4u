Quinoa Salad

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 1 hour 0 minutes

Servings: 1 serving

Ingredients:


2 avocadoes (ripe)
1/2 cup Baby Spinach
1/2 cup Cherry tomatoes
1/3 cup Red onion
1/3 cup Red onion
2 tbsp Lemon juice
1/2 cup Quinoa
1/4 tsp Pepper, ground
1/2 tsp Sea salt
2 tbsp Olive oil
1/3 cup Feta cheese
1 cup Water

Instructions:

Bring the quinoa to a boil with water until it's properly cooked and add it to a bowl. Add the chopped spinach, tomatoes, onion and cheese. Mix them all together. In a smaller bowl add water olive oil, pepper and salt and mix. Add the avocadoes and the dressing you just made. Lastly combine them and enjoy!
