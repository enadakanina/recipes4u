Spinach Borek

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 1 hour 0 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients:


Oil
Onion
Garlic
Spinach
Feta Cheese
Salt& Pepper
Tomato puree
Soaked cashews
Nutritional yeast (optional)
Yufka Pastry

Instructions:

To make the first filling for this albanian Borek you will start by frying Onion and Garlic in a pan with oil. Than wash, drain, dry and add the spinach. After it is fried mis it in a blender with feta and S+P and you're done. If you want you can make a second filling with tomato puree, soaked cashews, nutritipnal yeast is optional, S+P and garlic. Mix all of them in a blender and you're done. Grab the Yufka pastry and cut it in triangles. Fill the center with spinach or tomato filling and wrap it as instructed in the video. Brush it with oil, add sesame seeds and bake until the pastry is crispy. Enjoy!
