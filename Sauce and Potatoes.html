Whipped feta dip & crispy potatoes

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 15 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients:


8 oz. feta, crumbled
1/2 cup olive oil
1/4 cup whipped cream cheese
1/4 cup water
Lemon Zest
1tbst oregano
1-2tbsp honey
Potatoes
10 yukon potatoes
1/4 cup olive oil
1 tsp salt & pepper
1 tsp paprika

Instructions:

Boil potatoes and put them in a tray. Pour olive oil, salt, pepper and paprika on them and put in the oven until crispy. In a mixer add olive oil, whipped cream cheese, water, and feta cheese and blend. Add lemon zest oregano and honey and blend again. Mix the potatoes and the sauce, add olive oil and enjoy!
