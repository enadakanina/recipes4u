Raspberry Cake

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 1 hour 0 minutes

Servings: 16 servings

Ingredients:


1 cup Raspberries, fresh or thawed
3 Eggs, large
1/2 cup All-purpose flour, plain
1 cup Caster / super-fine sugar
1/2 cup Cocoa powder, unsweetened
200 g Dark chocolate
1/2 tsp Salt
1 tsp Vanilla essence
125 g Butter, unsalted

Instructions:

Grind dark chocolate into fine pieces and mix them with soft butter. Microwave for 1 minute. Add sugar and mix it all together. Add three eggs while mixing in between each pour. Add the vanilla extract and than the flour, salt and cocoa powder. Mix it all well. Add the raspberries and bake it for 35 minutes at 170 C. Enjoy!
