Stawberry Cheesecake Chimichanga

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 10 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Ingredients:


4 medium sized tortillas

8 oz cream cheese, room temp

1 cup chopped strawberries, plus more for filling

1/4 sugar

1/4 sour cream

2 tbsp melted butter

1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

2 tbsp granulated sugar

Instructions:

In a bowl add cream cheese, sour cream, and sugar. Mix until combined. Add the chopped berries and fold together. Add 3 tbsp in center with some strawberries. Fold in the sides and roll it into a burrito. Air fry for 10-12 minutes at 350 degrees. Brush with butter and roll in cinnamon sugar.
