Easter Salad

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 30 minutes

Servings: 1 serving

Ingredients:


Salad Greens
Strawberries
Goat Cheese
Silvered almonds
Olive Oil
Lemon juice
Honey
Sea Salt
Pepper
Poppy Seeds

Instructions:

Add salad Greens, sliced strawberries and goat cheese to a bowl. Than toast sivered almonds and add them to the bowl as well. For the dressing pour olive oil, lemon juice Honey, sea salt, pepper and poppy seads in a jar and shake it untik they mix together. Drizzle the dressing On the salad and that's it!
