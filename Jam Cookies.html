Jam Cookies

___________________________________________________________________________________________

Cook time: 0 hours 30 minutes

Servings: 28 servings

Ingredients:


1 Egg yolk

1/2 cup Jam

1/3 cup Cornflour / cornstarch

1/2 cup Icing sugar / confectioners sugar

1 1/2 cups Plain flour

1 pinch Salt

1 tsp Vanilla essence

200 g Butter, unsalted

Instructions:

Mix softened butter and powdered sugar together. Add an egg yolk and vanilla and mix again. Now add a pinch of salt, flour and cornstarch. Mix, knead to form a dough, shape them and fill them with jam. Bake them for 13-15 minutes at 180 degrees C or 355 F.
